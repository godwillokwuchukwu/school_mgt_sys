## 🏛️ Platform Overview

Riverside Academy School Management System is an enterprise educational management platform featuring:
- **School Problem Solver & Role-Gated AI Assistant**: Fine-tuned AI assistant connected directly to the database with strict role isolation (Admin, Teacher, Student, Parent).
- **Automated Admissions Lifecycle**: Seamless continuity from registration, document verification, school bank fee payment, and payment slip upload to official admission offer letters and automatic enrollment.
- **Institutional Multi-Domain Portal Accounts**: Automated provisioning of official domain credentials (`@student.riversideacademy.com`, `@staff.riversideacademy.com`, `@parent.riversideacademy.com`).
- **Academic & Administrative Management**: Comprehensive grading, attendance tracking, fee invoicing, and audit logging.
